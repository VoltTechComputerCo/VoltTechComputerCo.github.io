The upgrades people actually notice

Moving from a hard drive to an SSD can transform everyday responsiveness. More RAM can help multitasking. A better GPU can help gaming—but only when the rest of the system can support it.

Compatibility comes before buying

Motherboard support, power-supply capacity, physical clearance, cooling and the age of the other components all matter. A good upgrade should not create a new problem.

We can help you choose without overspending

Tell us your budget and what you want the PC to do better. We can help identify whether a targeted upgrade makes sense before you start buying parts.

Why we are pausing full custom builds

For now, VoltTech is focusing on repairs, diagnostics and upgrades rather than advertising full custom-PC build packages. That lets us quote more responsibly while the business grows.
