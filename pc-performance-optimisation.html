Why PCs slow down

Too many startup apps, a nearly full or failing drive, background software, overheating, low memory and Windows issues can all make a once-fast PC feel miserable.

What we check first

Startup behaviour, storage health, temperatures, obvious software clutter and system configuration. If there is a hardware problem underneath the slowness, that matters more than “optimising” around it.

What optimisation is not

We do not promise magical one-click speed boosts. Real improvement comes from fixing bottlenecks, removing unnecessary load and making sure the hardware can sustain normal performance.

When an upgrade is worth it

If the PC is healthy but limited by old storage, low RAM or another obvious bottleneck, we can explain which upgrade is likely to make a noticeable difference before you buy anything.
