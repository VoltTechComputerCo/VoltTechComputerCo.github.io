Delete my VoltTech account

Deletion removes your login and deletes or anonymises personal information VoltTech no longer needs. Transaction, tax, warranty, dispute or other records may still have to be retained where law requires it.

VoltTech uses a 7-day recovery window. During those 7 days you can cancel the deletion request. After the recovery period, deletion can be finalised and the login deletion is irreversible.
