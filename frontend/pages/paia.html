VOLTTECH / ACCESS TO INFORMATION

Information about access requests for records held by VoltTech and the formal compliance items that remain before release.

Self-service access

Customers can access many account records directly through My Account, Documents and Privacy & Data. The personal-data export is provided for convenience and does not replace a formal access request where one is appropriate.

Formal requests

Requests for access, correction or deletion can be sent to volttechcomputerco@gmail.com . Applicable PAIA and POPIA forms and guidance are published by the Information Regulator.

Typical records

Records may include customer correspondence, quotations and approvals, service records, invoices and order records, supplier records, account records and statutory or accounting records.

Official compliance resources

The Information Regulator provides the private-body PAIA manual template, PAIA manual submission service and Information Officer registration services.
