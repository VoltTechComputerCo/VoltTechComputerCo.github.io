// FAQ

Common questions

Can you help if I am outside Pretoria?

Yes. Streaming, OBS and creator software support is available remotely across South Africa where the work can reasonably be completed remotely. Physical PC repairs remain primarily Pretoria-based.

Do you only support Twitch?

No. We support Twitch, YouTube and other OBS-based workflows where practical.

Can you guarantee 1080p60?

No. Final quality depends on your PC, encoder capability, game workload, upload quality, platform limits and content. We optimise within the practical limits of the system.

Can you help with microphones and stream audio?

Yes. Basic stream-audio optimisation is part of Stream Tune and Stream Setup. More complex audio routing or specialised hardware may require a separately agreed scope.

What about capture cards or dual-PC setups?

We can assess creator setups involving capture hardware or multiple systems, but these are quoted according to complexity rather than treated as a standard package.

Will you tell me if I actually need a hardware upgrade?

Yes. If the system is genuinely hardware-limited, we explain the bottleneck and what type of upgrade would make a meaningful difference. We do not recommend replacing parts blindly.
