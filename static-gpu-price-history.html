A Brief, Enraging History of Gamers Getting Screwed by GPU Prices

GPU pricing has been shaped by mining booms, supply shortages, premium product positioning and the simple fact that enthusiasts keep paying more.

VOLTTECH COMPUTER CO. · 27 AUG 2026

The old midrange moved upward

Performance tiers that once felt comfortably mainstream gradually inherited premium price tags.

Crypto mining distorted supply

Mining demand repeatedly pulled cards out of gaming channels and made normal retail pricing feel fictional.

Shortages changed expectations

Pandemic-era supply problems taught buyers and manufacturers that much higher street prices could still clear the market.

Flagship pricing anchors everything below it

Once the top card becomes extremely expensive, the next tier down can look reasonable even when it also costs more than previous generations.

Value is now a generation-by-generation question

Brand loyalty matters less than checking real performance, power use, memory capacity and street price at the moment you buy.

The useful answer is usually less dramatic than the internet argument around it.
