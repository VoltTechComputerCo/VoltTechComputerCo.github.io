// WHAT WE FIX

Streaming problems are not all the same problem.

A stream can look terrible while the game itself feels perfect. OBS can report rendering lag, encoder overload or network dropped frames — and each points to a different part of the system. We work through the cause instead of throwing random bitrate and quality settings at it.

Encoding overloadCPU/GPU encoder load, output settings and encoder configuration.
Rendering lagGPU saturation, scene complexity, capture method and game/OBS contention.
Dropped framesUpload stability, bitrate, routing and connection-related loss.
Blurry or blocky videoResolution, FPS, bitrate and motion trade-offs.
Bad microphone audioGain, noise suppression, compressor, limiter and source balancing.
FPS drops while liveGame performance, OBS load, background processes, thermals and hardware limits.

// FAQ

Common questions

Can you help if I am outside Pretoria?

Yes. Streaming and OBS configuration can often be handled remotely anywhere in South Africa. Physical repairs remain primarily Pretoria-based.

Do you only support Twitch?

No. We support Twitch, YouTube and other OBS-based workflows where practical.

Can you guarantee 1080p60?

No. Final quality depends on your PC, encoder capability, game workload, upload quality, platform limits and content. We optimise within the limits of the system.

Will you tell me if I actually need an upgrade?

Yes. If the system is genuinely hardware-limited, we explain the bottleneck and the type of upgrade that would help.

Do I need to give you my password or stream key?

No. Do not send us passwords, 2FA codes or stream keys. Sensitive credentials stay with you.
