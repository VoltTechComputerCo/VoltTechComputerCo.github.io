    REMOTE SUPPORT · SOUTH AFRICA

    START WITH THE PROBLEM

    Streaming problems, without the preset roulette.

    Settings depend on the game, hardware, connection and platform. The useful first step is identifying whether the bottleneck is encoding, rendering, network, audio or system headroom.

    OBS setup & scenes

    Capture method, canvas/output choices, source structure and sensible scene configuration.

    Encoder overload

    NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync and x264 tuning when the encoding workload is too high.

    Dropped frames

    Bitrate, connection stability and the upload path when the stream cannot hold a clean connection.

    Audio cleanup

    Mic levels, noise control, compression, limiting, routing and source balance.

    Creator-PC performance

    Game-versus-stream resource contention, GPU headroom, thermals and background load.

    Capture & recording

    Capture-card setups, recording quality and combined streaming/recording workflows.

    QUICK ANSWERS

    Streaming support FAQ

    Do I need to be in Pretoria?

    No. Streaming and creator technical support is designed to work remotely across South Africa.

    Can you guarantee a specific stream quality?

    No. Final quality depends on hardware, game load, internet stability and platform limits. The goal is the best stable configuration the setup can realistically sustain.

    Do you need my Twitch or YouTube password?

    No. Never send passwords, 2FA codes or stream keys. Legitimate technical support does not require those credentials.
