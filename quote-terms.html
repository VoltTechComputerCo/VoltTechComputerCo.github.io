Quote Terms

Current version: 10 September 2026

These terms apply when a customer receives or accepts a VoltTech quotation through the customer account or another recorded channel.

1. What the quote covers

A quotation covers only the services, labour, parts, products, delivery charges and discounts shown on that quotation. The displayed total is the amount approved when the customer accepts it.

For repair or maintenance work, VoltTech will describe the proposed work and itemise charges as clearly as reasonably possible. The quote also shows its validity period.

2. Diagnosis and additional work

A fault can reveal additional problems after diagnosis or disassembly. VoltTech will not add chargeable work beyond the accepted quotation without informing the customer of the additional estimated charges and obtaining further approval.

If a separate diagnostic assessment carries a fee, that fee must be disclosed and approved before the diagnostic service is performed. A diagnostic service and a later repair quotation may therefore be separate items.

3. Parts, pricing and availability

Parts-based quotations depend on supplier price and stock at the time the quote is prepared. Unless a quotation has already been accepted and its commercial terms locked, VoltTech may withdraw or replace an expired quotation if supplier pricing or availability changes.

VoltTech does not currently present custom PC building as an established retail service. Any future special-order or system-build transaction will be separately confirmed before parts are ordered.

4. Acceptance

Pressing Accept quote records the customer account, quotation, accepted total, terms version and acceptance time. Acceptance authorises the scope shown on the quotation; it does not itself take payment.

Once accepted, VoltTech's system locks the commercial quote fields and line items to preserve the accepted record. If the scope must change, a new or revised quotation should be issued for approval.

5. Expiry

A quotation is valid until the date displayed on it unless VoltTech extends it in writing. An expired quotation may need to be re-priced before work or ordering begins.

6. Customer equipment and data

Customers should keep appropriate backups of important data before repair, upgrade, operating-system, malware-removal or storage work where reasonably possible. VoltTech will take reasonable care of equipment and data in its possession. Nothing in these terms excludes liability or customer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Statutory rights

These terms supplement, and do not replace, rights that may apply under the Consumer Protection Act, Electronic Communications and Transactions Act and other applicable South African law.
