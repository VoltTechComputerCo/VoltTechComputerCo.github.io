DLSS 5 Is Here — And It Is Starting to Change What ‘Rendering’ Means

NBA 2K27 is the first shipping game with Nvidia's DLSS 5 3D-Guided Neural Rendering. Instead of only reconstructing pixels, the model can alter lighting and material appearance under developer control.

7 September 2026 · STATIC

DLSS began as an answer to a performance problem: render fewer pixels, then use machine learning to reconstruct a convincing higher-resolution image. DLSS 5 pushes the idea somewhere more controversial. It can change the appearance of the scene itself.

Nvidia has released DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, and NBA 2K27 is its first shipping showcase on GeForce RTX 50-series GPUs. Nvidia says the model uses scene understanding plus developer guidance to improve lighting and materials — including skin, hair, fabric and contact shadows.

This is more than an upscaler

Nvidia describes DLSS 5 as a neural rendering model capable of adding effects such as subsurface scattering and more detailed light-material interactions, while developers retain controls for intensity, grading and masking.

STATIC signal: this is where “AI graphics” becomes an artistic-pipeline question, not just an FPS question. If a neural model changes material response and lighting, the debate becomes how much control artists keep — and whether the result still matches the visual direction they authored.

The launch matters more than the demo

A feature shipping in a real game is different from a stage demonstration. Developers and players can now judge it under ordinary camera motion, animation and gameplay rather than curated stills.
