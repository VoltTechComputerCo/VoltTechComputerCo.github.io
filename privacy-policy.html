Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

What we collect

VoltTech does not submit Signal Scan answers or contact fields to our own server. If you choose to send the generated WhatsApp or email message, that information is then handled by the service you use to send it.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand page visits, device types and broad engagement with the site. We also track actions such as Signal Scan starts and contact-link clicks so we can improve the website.

Customer enquiries

If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss repair options and manage any service you choose to book.

What we do not do

We do not sell customer information or use this website to collect payment-card details, ID numbers or passwords.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about information you have previously sent us or request deletion where applicable. You can also use browser controls and extensions to limit analytics tracking.
