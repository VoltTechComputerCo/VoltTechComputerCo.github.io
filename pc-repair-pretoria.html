// PC REPAIR & DIAGNOSTICS

PC repair for problems you cannot explain

If your computer will not start, keeps crashing, freezes, restarts or simply feels wrong, you do not need to know the cause. We start with the symptom and work backwards.

WhatsApp VoltTechRun Signal Scan

What we commonly diagnose

Power and boot failures, blue screens, random restarts, storage problems, overheating, unusual noises, failing components and Windows faults.

What “diagnostics” actually means

We test likely causes in a sensible order instead of replacing parts at random. The goal is to identify the real fault, explain it clearly and then discuss the most sensible repair path.

What you need to tell us

Very little. What happened just before the problem started, what you see on screen, any unusual sounds or smells, and whether the problem happens every time are all useful clues.

When a repair may not make sense

If a machine is very old or a major component has failed, the sensible answer may be to stop spending on it. We would rather tell you that than push an uneconomical repair.
