Lots of boring details become less boring when combined.

Web tracking can combine many small characteristics into a probabilistic fingerprint. This demo hashes a selection of the signals above plus a local canvas rendering. It is not guaranteed to be unique and VoltTech does not store it.

THE IMPORTANT PART

👋 We didn’t hack you.

Everything above came from browser features and information available to normal web content. That does not mean your PC is infected or insecure. It does show why browser privacy, permissions, updates and trusted websites matter.

We could not read your filesA normal webpage does not get arbitrary access to your documents, photos or drives.
We could not read your passwordsThis demo has no access to saved passwords, banking apps, messages or 2FA codes.
Your browser still revealed plentyDevice characteristics and permission state can be useful for both legitimate features and tracking.
OPTIONAL PERMISSION DEMO

Want to make it slightly scarier?

A webpage can ask for your location too — but modern browsers are supposed to put you in control. VoltTech will only request it if you press the button below. Your browser should show a real permission prompt unless you have already made a choice for this site.

Before you press itThe page does not currently have your coordinates just because the browser knows your timezone or language.
After you allow itThe browser can provide coordinates and an estimated accuracy to this page. The result is displayed locally.
