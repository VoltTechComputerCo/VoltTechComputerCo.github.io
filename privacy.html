Privacy Notice

Current version: 10 September 2026

This notice explains how VoltTech handles personal information used for enquiries, customer accounts, quotations, service work and future orders.

Information we may process

This may include your name, email address, phone number, suburb, saved delivery details, company name, preferred contact method, account identifiers, quotation and acceptance history, service history, order/payment status, messages and technical information reasonably needed to provide support.

Why we use it

VoltTech uses personal information to respond to enquiries, authenticate customer accounts, prepare and manage quotations, provide services, arrange collection/delivery, keep service and transaction records, prevent fraud or misuse, meet legal obligations and improve customer support.

Service providers

VoltTech uses third-party infrastructure to operate the website and account system, including GitHub Pages for website hosting, Supabase for account/database services and Google where a customer chooses Google sign-in. Payment providers may be added later. Providers may process information in other jurisdictions; VoltTech will use appropriate safeguards where required.

Security

VoltTech aims to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls and database row-level security. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so suspected incidents are investigated and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, customer support, transaction/service records, security, dispute handling and legal or accounting obligations. Records may be deleted or de-identified when no longer required.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information, object to certain processing, request deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy complaint. Account profile details can also be updated through the customer account where supported.

Contact and regulator

Privacy requests can be sent to volttechcomputerco@gmail.com. The South African Information Regulator provides complaint and compliance services through its official channels.
