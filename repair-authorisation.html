    Repair Authorisation

    VoltTech uses recorded approval so customers remain in control of diagnostic, repair and maintenance costs.

    Current version: 24 September 2026Pretoria, Gauteng · South Africa
    Legal centre Quote terms Terms Privacy PAIA Returns Delivery Repair approval

    Initial assessment

    Where preparing a diagnostic assessment or estimate itself has a charge, VoltTech will disclose that charge before performing the chargeable assessment and obtain the customer's approval. An assessment may identify further work requiring a separate quotation.

    Authorising work

    Accepting a VoltTech repair quotation authorises only the work and charges listed on that quotation. The account system records the quotation and approval information associated with the transaction.

    Additional work

    If further problems are discovered, VoltTech will explain the additional work and estimated charge and obtain further recorded approval before proceeding where required. A revised or additional quotation may be issued so the new scope is clear.

    Declining additional work

    A customer may decline additional work. Any already-authorised diagnostic or completed work remains payable according to the agreed scope and applicable law.

    Data and passwords

    Repair work can involve storage devices, operating systems and personal files. VoltTech will only request access reasonably needed for the service and customers should back up important information where reasonably possible.
