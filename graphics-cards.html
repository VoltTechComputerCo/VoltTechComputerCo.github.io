    BUYING GUIDE

    Pick a GPU for the way you play

    1080p

    Start with what you actually play. A card that suits esports at competitive settings may struggle with a newer single-player game at the same refresh rate. If you’re chasing high frame rates, check CPU performance too.

    1440p

    Compare benchmarks at 1440p for your games and settings. High-resolution textures and ray tracing can make a big difference, so check video memory and performance with the settings you plan to use.

    4K

    Decide whether you want native 4K or are happy to use upscaling. Compare results at that setting and budget for the power supply and cooling your chosen card needs.

    Before you order

    Check card length and slot thickness against your case, especially if you have a front radiator. Confirm your power supply’s wattage and available connectors against the exact card model, and check that the card has the ports your monitor needs.

    Need a second opinion?

    Send VoltTech your CPU, power supply model, case and monitor resolution, plus the games you play and your budget. We can help narrow down the options.
