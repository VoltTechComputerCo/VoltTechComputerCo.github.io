    VOLTTECH / CREATOR REGISTRATION

    Add your Twitch channel.

    South African creator? Connect Twitch once and your channel can be added to the VoltTech Creator Hub automatically. You do not need a VoltTech customer account.

    01Twitch confirms you own the channel. 02You confirm you are a South African creator. 03Your channel joins the Creator Hub automatically.

    CONNECT

    Twitch-verified registration.

    VoltTech uses Twitch sign-in only to verify the channel identity and load the public Twitch profile used in Creator Hub. Your Twitch password is never shared with VoltTech.

    By connecting Twitch, you acknowledge the VoltTech Privacy Notice. No Twitch access token is stored after registration.

    NO VOLTTECH ACCOUNT?

    No problem.

    You can register only as a Creator. If you already have a VoltTech account and are signed in, the Twitch channel is linked to that account automatically.

    ALREADY LISTED?

    Connect anyway.

    Signing in with Twitch verifies channel ownership and refreshes your public Twitch profile details in VoltTech.
